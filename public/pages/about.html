OUR STORY

18 Years of Precision Automotive Service

Founded in 2008, MOTORWORKS began as a single-bay garage with a simple promise: honest diagnostics, precision repairs, and no upselling. What started as one mechanic's vision has grown into a state-of-the-art 12-bay facility serving thousands of loyal customers across the Chicago area.

Over the past 18 years, we've invested in the latest diagnostic equipment, expanded our team of ASE-certified technicians, and built a reputation rooted in transparency and technical excellence.

Today, MOTORWORKS is one of the most trusted independent service centers in the region, combining old-school craftsmanship with modern technology to keep your vehicle running at its best.

Our Mission

To provide transparent, precision automotive service that empowers every vehicle owner to make informed decisions about their car's health. We deliver honest diagnostics, quality repairs, and fair pricing — every time, for every customer.

Our Vision

To be the most trusted independent automotive service center in the nation — recognized for our unwavering commitment to integrity, technical excellence, and customer experience that redefines what car care should feel like.
